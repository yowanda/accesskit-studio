# Demo Video Script — AccessKit Studio (90–120 seconds)

## Script

| Section | Time | What to say / show |
|---------|------|--------------------|
| Hook + intro | 0:00–0:10 | "Hi, we're Team AccessKit, and this is AccessKit Studio — one campaign brief becomes an accessible marketing kit in under a minute." (Show home screen.) |
| Problem | 0:10–0:25 | "A nonprofit posted a beautiful poster — and a blind supporter couldn't read it. No alt text, no captions. Small teams don't ignore accessibility; they just don't have time or experts." |
| Solution | 0:25–0:45 | "Type a brief, and AccessKit generates the post, captions, poster, alt text, a plain-language version, and a captioned video script — then audits it all with a live accessibility score." (Scroll the brief form.) |
| Live demo | 0:45–1:30 | "Sample brief is loaded. I click Generate… here's the kit. Here's the audit — 86 out of 100, seven real checks. Now watch: I weaken the alt text to one letter… the score drops. It's a real check, not a badge. I fix it, tick the four human-review boxes, and export the kit." (Narrate every click. Move the cursor slowly.) |
| Closing | 1:30–1:45 | "Accessibility in the draft, not bolted on at the end. Thanks for watching!" |

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Do Not Disturb ON, notifications closed
- [ ] Bookmarks bar + extra tabs hidden
- [ ] App pre-loaded with the sample brief ("Free Coding Class for Teens")
- [ ] Quiet room, mic close
- [ ] One 15-second test recording, listened back

## Editing & upload

- Trim dead air at start/end (CapCut / iMovie / Clipchamp)
- 1080p · MP4 · under 2 minutes · check audio on final export
- Upload to YouTube → **Unlisted** (NOT Private) → verify the link in an incognito window
- Paste the link in the Devpost submission and the main README
