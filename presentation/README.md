# Presentation — AccessKit Studio (8 slides, ~3 min)

## Slide 1 — Title (10s)

**AccessKit Studio — Accessible Campaigns in Minutes**
One brief → an accessible marketing kit. Team + repo QR.

## Slide 2 — Problem (30s)

"A small nonprofit posted a beautiful event poster — and a blind supporter replied that they had no idea what it said. No alt text. No plain-language version. No captions."
Small teams don't ship inaccessible content from indifference. They ship it because accessibility feels like a separate, expert-only job that comes after publishing.

Say: "Accessibility shouldn't be a filter at the end. It should be in the draft."

## Slide 3 — Solution (30s)

"AccessKit Studio helps nonprofits, small businesses, and creators turn one campaign brief into an accessible marketing kit — in under a minute."
Post, captions, poster, alt text, plain-language version, captioned video script — plus a live accessibility score and a human review gate.

## Slide 4 — Demo (60s)

Live: type nothing (sample brief pre-loaded) → click Generate → scroll the kit → point at the audit score → edit the alt text to one letter → score drops live → tick 4 review boxes → export.
Narrate every click. "I generate… the kit appears… the audit scores it… I weaken the alt text and the score drops — it's a real check, not a badge."

## Slide 5 — How it works (20s)

Simple diagram: Brief → Generation (built-in engine + optional Gemini) → Audit (WCAG contrast math, reading level, platform rules) → Human review gate → Export. Static hosting on GitHub Pages; no user data leaves the browser except the optional AI call.

## Slide 6 — Impact (15s)

"Turns a 2-hour accessibility retrofit into a 1-minute draft — with the score to prove it."
Who benefits: nonprofits, UMKM/small businesses, creators, community groups — the teams with the least budget and the most to gain from being reachable by everyone.

## Slide 7 — What's next (10s)

PNG poster export with embedded alt text · more platforms + languages · team workspaces + brand kits · auto .srt captions.

## Slide 8 — Team + Thanks (5s)

Names, roles, repo QR code, live demo URL.

---

## Design rules

- Max ~20 words per slide. Screenshots > text.
- Big font (24pt+), high contrast, consistent colors.
- Spell-check the title slide.

## Demo insurance

- Record a backup screen capture of the happy path before presenting.
- Have the app already open with the sample brief loaded.
- If Wi-Fi dies, narrate over the backup video.

## Q&A prep

- "What was the hardest technical part?" → Making the audit honest: every check recomputes live from edited text.
- "How is this different from existing tools?" → Others generate content OR check accessibility. AccessKit does both in one flow, with a human gate before export.
- "Who would pay / who's the user?" → Nonprofits, small businesses, creators — freemium for basic kits, team/brand-kit plans later.
- "What would you build next?" → PNG export, more platforms/languages, team workspaces.
